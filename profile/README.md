## About us

**Pomnex** is a technology company dedicated to operating and managing WordPress systems. We don't sell themes and we don't rent out hosting. We take on the hard part: keeping your site secure, fast, and available every single day.

Most WordPress sites don't break because of poor design. They break because nobody is operating them: a deferred update, a backup no one ever tested, a vulnerability in an old plugin, an alert nobody read. So we treat every site as a live production system that needs continuous monitoring, maintenance, and hardening, not a project that ships once and is left alone.

We work with individuals, businesses, and agencies that can't afford downtime: a store that sells every day, a platform its clients depend on, or an agency running dozens of client sites that needs a technical partner willing to share the responsibility.

## Vision, mission, and purpose

- **Vision:** Make "always running" the default standard for every WordPress site, and make Pomnex the reference for WordPress operations, regionally and globally.
- **Mission:** Take on the responsibility of keeping our clients' sites secure, fast, and available, through maintenance, monitoring, and custom development held to clear engineering standards and full transparency, so they can focus on their business.
- **Purpose:** Reduce downtime and risk to the lowest possible level: know a site is down before anyone has to tell us, ship updates without surprises, and give every client a fast human response.

## How we work

1. **Assessment & ops plan:** We assess the site's state, risks, and operational needs, and build a clear management plan on top of that.
2. **Develop & configure:** We configure the site and build any plugins or systems it needs, with clean, scalable code.
3. **Go live & monitor:** We harden the system and connect continuous monitoring from day one.
4. **Ongoing care:** We monitor, maintain, and catch issues early, and report what we did in plain language.

## Our engineering standards

Professionalism, for us, is a set of daily practices we hold on every project:

| Area | Our commitment |
|---|---|
| **Security** | Least privilege for every account and system, no passwords or keys in code or repositories, and a security review for every plugin before handover. |
| **Code review** | No change reaches production without a merge request reviewed by another engineer. |
| **Automated checks** | A CI/CD pipeline runs quality checks and builds on every change before it merges, and deployments are repeatable, not manual. |
| **Backups** | A fresh backup before any significant change, and regular restore tests. A backup that has never been restored doesn't count. |
| **Documentation** | Every system we deliver is documented well enough for another developer to pick it up without us. |
| **Incidents** | When something breaks, we tell the client what happened, why, and what we did about it, and we record the lesson so it doesn't happen again. |
| **Privacy** | We collect as little data as possible and use it only for its purpose, as described in our [Privacy Policy](https://pomnex.com/en/privacy-policy). |
| **Bilingual by default** | Our interfaces and products support English and Arabic, with full right-to-left layout support. |
